CONTROLS

TOUCH (ANDROID)

  • Left side - drag to move. Push the stick to its edge to sprint.
  • Right side - drag to look around.
  • USE - take items, read, open, hide. Some actions need you to HOLD.
  • FLASH - flashlight on / off. It drains a battery.
  • CROUCH - toggle. Crouching is quiet and harder to see.
  • SPRINT - hold, or push the stick to the edge. Loud!
  • RADIO - tune stations; BAG - items & documents.
  • While hiding: BREATH (hold) and LEAVE.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE (TESTING)

  • W A S D move · Shift sprint · C crouch
  • Mouse look (click the game to capture) · F flashlight
  • E / Space use (hold) · R answer a voice · B hold breath · Q leave hiding
  • Tab / I bag · Esc / P pause

SURVIVAL

  • It is blind but hears everything. Footsteps, doors, the radio, the generator.
  • Break line of sight, then hide in a cabinet and hold your breath.
  • If you hear your own voice: do not answer.
